Kosh privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Kosh is a personal finance app for tracking expenses, budgets, goals and debts.

Gmail access

  • If you choose Connect Gmail, Kosh asks Google for read-only access (gmail.readonly).
  • Kosh only searches emails from bank and card alert senders, and reads them on your iPhone. Emails are never sent to any server run by Kosh.
  • From each alert, Kosh keeps only the amount, merchant, date and bank name, and shows them to you for review. The email itself is not stored. Card numbers, account numbers and other identifiers are never kept.
  • Kosh never sends, changes or deletes email, and never shares Gmail data with anyone or uses it for advertising.
  • Kosh's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Kosh (More → Connect Gmail → Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your finance data

Entries live on your device. If you turn on sync, they are stored in your own private database, readable only by your login.
